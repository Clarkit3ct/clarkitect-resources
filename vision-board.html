Tool 02

The Vision Board Maker

Ten questions about the life you're building. You get a one-page plan for it, written in your own words, plus the exact prompts that turn it into a script, an image, and a nightly audio practice.

What this actually makes Architects don't make mood boards. They make drawing sets: the documents you build from, posted where the builders can see them. This makes one of those for your life. One page, your words, no stock photos of somebody else's boat.
STEP 01

Answer

Ten short questions, one at a time. About ten minutes. Scenes and feelings, not goals.

STEP 02

Get your sheet

A one-page plan you can print, save as a PDF, or bookmark with its own link.

STEP 03

Make it audible

Copy your two prompts. Any AI writes the script. A free voice tool reads it. You listen nightly.

01 · You

First name

Your sheet gets titled with it.

ExampleJess
02 · The frame

How far ahead?

Close enough to feel real. Far enough to be built.

ExampleJess picked 5 years, so her sheet is dated 2031.
3 years out5 years out10 years out
03 · The scene

It's 6 AM there

You step outside. What do you see, hear, feel underfoot? Write it as if it's happening now. What's the first sound?

ExampleThe grass is wet and I can hear the creek past the garden. Steam coming off the coffee. Nobody needs anything from me for another hour.
04 · The place

Where is this?

Town, land, water. Real place or one you can picture.

ExampleA timber house outside Sandpoint, Idaho
05 · The work

Your hands, Tuesday afternoon

Not your job title. What are your hands actually doing?

ExampleSanding a cedar bench for the sauna, with the sketch for the next one pinned above the vise
06 · The people

Who's at your table?

Dinner, in that life. Use their real names.

ExampleSam, the two kids, and whoever wandered over from next door
07 · The number

Write the number

Income, savings, or the figure you've quietly carried for years. Use your real number, not a bigger one. The believable one is the one that works.

Example$1,200,000
08 · The state

Pick three feelings

How it feels in your body inside that life. Breathe once before you choose.

ExampleJess picked grounded, playful, grateful.
groundedfreeabundantcalmdrivenplayfulconnectedstronggratefulwonder
09 · The line

9 PM. One line in the journal.

What did that day deserve? One sentence.

ExampleToday was slow in the best way.
10 · How you build

Which one is more you?

Everybody builds differently. This gets stamped on your sheet, the way a drawing is signed by whoever drew it. Skip it if none fit.

The ArchitectYou want the whole plan before you start. The MakerYou figure it out by building the thing. The OperatorYou win with systems and steady cadence. The SparkYou move fast on instinct and momentum. The StudentYou go deep, understand it, then act.

The Practice Kit

01Print this sheet, or save it as a PDF, and put it where the day starts. The desk, the mirror, the shop wall. Drawings belong where the builders can see them.

02Copy Prompt 1 into any AI. Answer its questions about your real past. It writes an 800-word profile of your future life, reported like a news story. Read it once and fix anything that isn't true to you, especially the quotes.

03Make a free ElevenLabs account. Text to Speech. Paste your script. Pick a calm documentary narrator, never your own voice, and test three before you choose. Model: Multilingual v2. Stability 55. Style near zero. Speed 0.95. Download the MP3. The free tier covers a five-minute track.

04Put it on your phone and listen every night for 30 nights. In bed, low volume. Three slow breaths before you press play, then listen from inside the life, not outside it. Don't re-cut it for 30 days. Repetition is the practice.

05Prompt 2 renders your 6 AM scene. Set it as a wallpaper, or print it beside the sheet.

06Watch what makes you tear up, in the script or in your own answers. That line is pointing at something true. Full craft notes are in Protocol 01: The Future Self Audio.
