Three rounds of breath. In through the nose... and let it go. Again — fill the whole chest... and release. One more — all the way in... hold at the top... and let it fall away.

Now a hand on your heart. Say it with me — in the body, not just the mouth.

Thank you, thank you, thank you — for the property. The land that fuels my creativity and asks me to be its voice. It teaches me, it holds me, and it ties all the way back home, to the Clark farm.

Thank you, thank you, thank you — for the team. For the crew and the vessel, and the precious cargo we carry.

Thank you, thank you, thank you — for Hattie. For the deepening. For a home that can truly hold a family.

Thank you, thank you, thank you — for the stream. Money moves with no resistance. I am generous without fear. Calm without vigilance.

Thank you for these hands and this mind — and for everything they're still learning.

Now come with me, because this is real.

It's early. You step off the porch and the grass is wet under your feet. You can hear water — it was a requirement, remember, and there it is. Fog is sitting in the low field where the creek bends. The best room on the property. Somewhere behind you the kids are laughing at a chore that turned into a game. The shop is warm and there's a sketch on the bench waiting for you. Nothing is chasing you. The systems are holding. The people are capable. You built a container, and it's beautiful, and it was made on purpose.

Feel that in your chest. Not someday. Now.

You are the source. Your state is a decision, and you've already made it.

It's already done.

Now — go build like it.
