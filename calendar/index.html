The whole year
on one wall

The Clarity Calendar is a 48 by 36 sheet with every day of the year on it. Each month is a single row, weekends shaded, holidays marked, room to write in every square. When the year hangs in front of you, you plan differently.

Make your own Get the 2027 PDF
2027 · One sheet · 48 × 36
HOW

Wall in three steps

01

Set it up

Use the ready 2027 PDF, or open the Maker and pick your year, turn holidays on or off, and add your own dates so they print on the sheet.

02

Print it big

Any print shop can run it. Ask for 48 by 36 at 100%. Color is best; a black and white engineering print works and costs a few dollars.

03

Hang it where you plan

Office, studio, kitchen wall. A pen on a string next to it. Write commitments in the squares, not in an app.

Why paper, why this big? A screen shows you a month at a time and lets you scroll away from your promises. A wall shows you the shape of the whole year, how close October really is, and what you said you would do by then.
